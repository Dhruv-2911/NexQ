# Changelog

## [0.1.0] (2026-11-12)

### Features

* Realtime transcription of both sides of a meeting: your microphone and the other side's audio, each with its own speech-to-text provider
* Start and stop a meeting from the dashboard, the overlay, the tray or a shortcut
* Meeting history with saved transcripts, search and export
* Live translation of the transcript
* Hide from screen share keeps the overlay out of screen shares and recordings
